## Core Concepts & Key Ideas

Here are the step-by-step concepts to keep in mind when starting your project:

1. **Locate the center of your plot (Brahmasthan) using a magnetic compass to accurately map all cardinal directions.**: This forms the foundation of your plan. Make sure to assess room dimensions and utility loads.
2. **Position the main entrance door in the auspicious North, East, or Northeast zones of the facade.**: Focus on quality over short-term savings. Choosing the right material grade prevents structural wear.
3. **Design the kitchen in the Southeast (Agneya) corner, placing the cooking hob facing East.**: Optimize spaces by using sliding tracks, recessed wall niches, and hidden organizers.
4. **Locate the master bedroom in the Southwest (Nairutya) corner to establish stability and leadership.**: Lay down layered lighting systems. Warm temperatures make rooms feel inviting and luxurious.
5. **Ensure water sumps and borewells are dug in the Northeast (Ishanya) zone, never in the Southwest.**: Align room doors, windows, and heavy furniture placements with orientation Vastu guidelines.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many homeowners run into unexpected budgets and structural repairs due to simple errors. Here is what to avoid:

- **Placing a toilet or septic tank in the Northeast corner, which destroys positive mental energies.**: This ruins spatial flows and blocks natural daylight corridors.
- **Positioning the kitchen directly below or above a toilet or master bedroom bed location.**: Buying low-cost materials leads to sagging shelves and water leakage issues later on.
- **Building an internal staircase in the Northeast quadrant of the house, blocking light and energy flows.**: Neglecting ventilation traps moisture and encourages mold growth behind wardrobes.
- **Having the main entrance door face a direct obstruction like a street T-junction or electrical transformer.**: Overlooking local society or government guidelines can halt your construction project.
- **Keeping the center of the house (Brahmasthan) heavy by building columns or walls there.**: Forgetting to budget for essential hidden costs like sumps, compound gates, and safety grills.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### FAQ Accordion

#### Q1: What is the best Vastu remedy for a kitchen placed in the North direction?
**A:** A kitchen in the North is a Vastu defect. As a remedy, place a green marble slab under the gas stove, avoid yellow/red color schemes, and paint the kitchen walls in light shades of blue or green.

#### Q2: Can we have a pooja room inside the master bedroom?
**A:** Vastu advises against placing a pooja room inside a bedroom. If space is tight, ensure the pooja mandir has doors, is placed in the Northeast corner of the room, and is closed during sleeping hours.

#### Q3: Where should overhead water tanks be placed on the terrace?
**A:** Overhead water tanks are heavy structures and should strictly be placed in the Southwest or West zone of the terrace to balance the building's load layout.

#### Q4: What is the Vastu rule for main door size?
**A:** The main door should always be the largest door in the house, ideally constructed with high-quality teak wood and made of two door shutters opening inwards.

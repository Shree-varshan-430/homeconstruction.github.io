## Core Concepts & Key Ideas

Here are the step-by-step concepts to keep in mind when starting your project:

1. **Measure the room's height and width, leaving at least 3 feet of clearance in front of swing-door wardrobes.**: This forms the foundation of your plan. Make sure to assess room dimensions and utility loads.
2. **Construct the carcass with 18mm Boiling Water Resistant (BWR) plywood to support heavy shelf loads.**: Focus on quality over short-term savings. Choosing the right material grade prevents structural wear.
3. **Opt for sliding door systems with aluminium profiles if the corridor between the bed and closet is narrow.**: Optimize spaces by using sliding tracks, recessed wall niches, and hidden organizers.
4. **Incorporate internal drawers with soft-close runners for undergarments, watches, and jewelry storage.**: Lay down layered lighting systems. Warm temperatures make rooms feel inviting and luxurious.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many homeowners run into unexpected budgets and structural repairs due to simple errors. Here is what to avoid:

- **Using thin 12mm plywood for shelves, which bends and sags under the weight of stacked clothes.**: This ruins spatial flows and blocks natural daylight corridors.
- **Choosing cheap, non-branded sliding tracks that derail or lock up within a few months.**: Buying low-cost materials leads to sagging shelves and water leakage issues later on.
- **Neglecting ventilation, leading to damp smells inside dark, closed cabinet corners.**: Neglecting ventilation traps moisture and encourages mold growth behind wardrobes.
- **Forgetting to design a full-length mirror panel, requiring a separate dressing console.**: Overlooking local society or government guidelines can halt your construction project.
- **Forgetting to allocate taller hanger rods for Indian long ethnic wear like sarees and coats.**: Forgetting to budget for essential hidden costs like sumps, compound gates, and safety grills.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### FAQ Accordion

#### Q1: What is the standard depth of a master bedroom wardrobe?
**A:** The standard depth of a wardrobe is 24 inches (2 feet) to allow standard clothes hangers to fit comfortably without catching on doors. For sliding wardrobes, a depth of 26 inches is recommended to accommodate the track mechanism.

#### Q2: Is MDF good for wardrobe shutters?
**A:** MDF is suitable for decorative shutters with CNC grooving, but it is heavy and sensitive to water. For structural frames and interior shelves, BWR plywood is far superior in strength and durability.

#### Q3: How can I design a cost-effective walk-in wardrobe?
**A:** To save costs, use open cabinet frames without doors in a dedicated dressing room. Combine standard laminates for internal carcass boxes with premium veneers on external frames.

#### Q4: What are the best hinges for kitchen and wardrobe doors?
**A:** We highly recommend using clip-on soft-close hydraulic auto-hinges from reputed brands like Hettich, Blum, Hafele, or Ebco.

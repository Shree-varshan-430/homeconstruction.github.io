## Core Concepts & Key Ideas

Here are the step-by-step concepts to keep in mind when starting your project:

1. **Analyze the work triangle (Sink, Hob, Fridge) and verify that the path between them is unobstructed.**: This forms the foundation of your plan. Make sure to assess room dimensions and utility loads.
2. **Use BWR plywood (marine grade) for all wet-zone under-sink cabinets to avoid water rot.**: Focus on quality over short-term savings. Choosing the right material grade prevents structural wear.
3. **Install soft-close tandem boxes and pull-outs instead of standard open shelves for easy cabinet access.**: Optimize spaces by using sliding tracks, recessed wall niches, and hidden organizers.
4. **Choose quartz or premium granite for the countertop, selecting scratch-resistant and non-porous slabs.**: Lay down layered lighting systems. Warm temperatures make rooms feel inviting and luxurious.
5. **Incorporate high-suction auto-clean chimneys (1200+ m3/hr suction) to exhaust deep-fry smoke.**: Align room doors, windows, and heavy furniture placements with orientation Vastu guidelines.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many homeowners run into unexpected budgets and structural repairs due to simple errors. Here is what to avoid:

- **Using cheap particle board or MDF for cabinets, which swells up and disintegrates when wet.**: This ruins spatial flows and blocks natural daylight corridors.
- **Failing to plan electrical points for micro-appliances like mixers, water purifiers, and ovens.**: Buying low-cost materials leads to sagging shelves and water leakage issues later on.
- **Installing poor-quality drawer slides that jam or break under heavy steel spice boxes.**: Neglecting ventilation traps moisture and encourages mold growth behind wardrobes.
- **Creating narrow walking corridors in parallel kitchen layouts (minimum width should be 4 feet).**: Overlooking local society or government guidelines can halt your construction project.
- **Placing the gas stove directly next to the sink, violating basic kitchen safety and Vastu rules.**: Forgetting to budget for essential hidden costs like sumps, compound gates, and safety grills.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### FAQ Accordion

#### Q1: What is the difference between modular kitchen shutters finished in laminates vs acrylic?
**A:** Laminates are highly scratch-resistant, cost-effective, and available in textured/matte finishes. Acrylic shutters offer a premium, mirror-like high-gloss finish, are extremely easy to clean, but cost about 30% more and show fingerprint smudges easily.

#### Q2: Which countertop is better for Indian cooking: Quartz or Granite?
**A:** Granite is natural, heat-resistant, and virtually indestructible, but has limited color options. Quartz is engineered, non-porous, stain-resistant, and comes in beautiful marble-like designs, though it is slightly sensitive to direct hot pans.

#### Q3: How can I design a termite-proof modular kitchen?
**A:** Ensure the carcass is constructed using 100% Boiling Water Proof (BWP) plywood or PVC foam boards for the sink area. Treat the walls with anti-termite chemicals before installing cabinets.

#### Q4: What is the ideal distance between the countertop and wall cabinets?
**A:** The standard distance is 24 inches (2 feet) to ensure comfortable cooking clearance and correct installation height for chimneys and dish racks.
